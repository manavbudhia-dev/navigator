Sourdough starter: a two-week guide

Flour, water, patience. Feed equal weights of whole rye and bread flour twice a day, keep the jar at 24 degrees, and by day nine the culture doubles in four hours.

If the surface grows a grey fuzz, discard it, tighten the lid and start again from a spoon of the culture underneath.

A long cold retard in the fridge develops the tang most people are actually chasing when they bake at home.

Bake at 240 degrees with steam for twenty minutes, then drop to 210 until the crust sounds hollow when you knock it.

Store the loaf cut side down on a board; a plastic bag turns the crackling crust soft within an hour.

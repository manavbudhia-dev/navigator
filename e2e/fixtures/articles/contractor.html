Retrofit contractor notes: the honest answer on heat pump savings

Customers ask whether a heat pump saves money on the heating bill, and the honest answer is that the machine saves money and a bad installation gives it back.

In our 90 retrofits the best heating bill saving came from houses that paired the heat pump with a heat pump water heater, cutting total utility cost by a third.

Radiator upsizing matters more than brand: a cold climate heat pump feeding old radiators at 60 degrees spends most of its efficiency advantage and the bill reflects it.

We log the heating bill for every job for two winters, and the spread between the best and worst saving in cold climates is wider than the average saving itself.

Commissioning, not the heat pump, is where the money is: a balanced flow and a 45 degree design temperature turn a mediocre result into a real cut in the heating bill.

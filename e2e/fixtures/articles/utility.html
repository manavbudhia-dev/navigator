Utility programme evaluation: heat pump bill savings for 4,800 homes

We compared twelve months of metered energy before and after a heat pump retrofit in 4,800 homes and normalised every heating bill to the same weather.

The average home cut its heating bill by 31 percent. Homes replacing oil or propane cut more than half, while homes replacing a modern gas boiler saved close to nothing.

Total energy cost, including the electricity used for cooling, fell 18 percent, because a heat pump also replaces the air conditioner in the same machine.

Rebates changed the payback math far more than the savings did: the median payback was 6 years with a rebate and 14 years without one.

A fifth of the homes in the programme saw no bill reduction at all, and the audit team traced nearly every one of them to an oversized or badly commissioned heat pump.
